A small team that knows your street

Brightpaw started in Mueller in 2026 with one leash, a lot of early mornings and a simple idea: a dog should see the same friendly face every walk, and the owner should know exactly how it went.

We stay small on purpose. Our walkers cover four neighborhoods they live in or next to, so nobody is stuck in traffic while your dog waits by the door.

Our promise

  • The same walker every week, introduced in person first.
  • A report after every visit: route, time, water and bathroom notes.
  • On time, or the walk is free.
  • Heat days handled: earlier walks, shade and extra water.
