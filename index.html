How a first walk works

Three steps, and most dogs are walking with us inside a week.

Meet your walker

A free 20-minute visit at your place. Your walker meets your dog, learns the routine and picks up a key.

Pick your days

Choose weekdays and a time window. Walks are booked a week ahead and you can change them until 8 pm the night before.

Get a report

After every walk you get the route, the time, water and bathroom notes, and a line from your walker.

Walks close to home

We keep our walkers inside four neighborhoods so they're never far from your door and never late.

  • Hyde Park weekdays, 7 am to 6 pm

  • East Austin weekdays, 8 am to 5 pm
